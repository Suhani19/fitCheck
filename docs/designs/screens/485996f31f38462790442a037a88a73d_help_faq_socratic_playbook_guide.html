Eliminating Mic Clipping & Room Echo

When speech hits 0 dBFS, digital clipping strips phonetic nuance, causing the AI transcription parser to misinterpret technical keywords.

check_small Set hardware interface preamp (Focusrite/Elgato) so loudest peaks touch -12 dBFS, never 0 dBFS.
check_small For Shure MV7 / Rode NT-USB, keep capsule 3–4 inches from mouth at a 45-degree off-axis angle.
check_small Wear closed-back headphones to avoid open-speaker acoustic bleed back into the microphone.

Telemetry & Scoring Methodology FAQ

Everything you need to know about the FitCheck algorithmic scoring breakdown, data governance, and audio retention.

The overall composite score is not a subjective mood rating. It is a strictly weighted index across 4 quantitative dimensions calibrated against over 12,000 recorded L6/L7 bar-raiser sessions at Stripe, Meta, and Google:

30% WEIGHT Invariants & Correctness Proof of data integrity, CAP tradeoffs, partition boundaries, and fault containment.
20% WEIGHT Quantitative Precision Concrete back-of-the-envelope calculations, QPS, IOPS, network byte bounds, and storage wear.
25% WEIGHT Pushback Rigor & Composure Ability to hold architectural ground under hostile probing without defensive anger or hand-waving.
25% WEIGHT Structural Pacing & Flow Speech rate adherence (135–155 WPM), minimal filler tokens, and 1.2s silence reset after interruption.

Acoustic analysis in executive hiring shows that speaking above 160 WPM projects physiological stress, panic, and lack of message synthesis under confrontation. Conversely, speaking below 120 WPM is perceived as hesitation or conceptual searching.

The 135–155 WPM bracket creates a cadence of deliberate authority. FitCheck's real-time studio teleprompter displays dynamic pacing alerts if your running 10-second vocal average deviates beyond these safe thresholds.
